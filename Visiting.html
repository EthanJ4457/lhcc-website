You are welcome here

We’d love to welcome you.

Whether you’re exploring faith, new to the area, or looking for a church family, there is a place for you at Living Hope.

What to expect

Our gatherings are Christ-centered and welcoming, shaped by people from many cultures and backgrounds. You’ll experience Scripture, prayer, worship, and a community learning to follow Jesus together.

Families & children

There’s room for every generation.

We’re glad to welcome families and children at Living Hope. Children are welcome in our worship service, and we are growing in how we support families as part of our church community.

When & where

Join us this Sunday.

We gather weekly at 209 Cochrane Street in Whitby, Ontario. Services are family-friendly and relaxed. You’re welcome to come as you are.

Sunday worship 1:30 PM 209 Cochrane St. Whitby, ON L1N 5H9

Getting connected

We’d love to meet you.

If you have questions before your visit or would like to connect with someone from our community, we’d love to hear from you.
