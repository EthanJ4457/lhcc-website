Guyana Mission

Across Guyana, pastors and church leaders are hungry for biblical training, practical tools, and spiritual encouragement. Over the next four years, we are working alongside churches and leaders to provide Timothy Leadership Training that strengthens leaders, equips churches, and supports long-term ministry growth.

This is not just a one-time trip. It is a commitment to develop leaders who will train others, creating lasting impact for generations in their communities. We believe that when leaders are equipped, the church is strengthened and communities are transformed.
